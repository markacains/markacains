# Hi, I'm Mark Cains 👋

**Solutions Architect & Contact Center Engineer** with 20+ years of experience. I architect enterprise contact center ecosystems that handle millions of interactions — reliably, securely, and at scale.

I lead full-lifecycle CCaaS migrations from legacy platforms (Cisco UCCX/UCCE, Avaya, Mitel, NEC, NICE CXone) to **Genesys Cloud CX** across federal, healthcare, and enterprise environments. I've designed and maintained 100+ contact centers processing over 1 million calls a day for one of the largest U.S. government agencies. U.S. Army Veteran.

## What I do

- **Architecture** — End-to-end Genesys Cloud CX design: IVR / Conversational AI, omnichannel routing, WEM/WFM, speech analytics, and API / Data Action integrations
- **Migration leadership** — Pilot-to-scale roadmaps, number-porting strategy, complex call-flow translation, and cutovers with zero critical downtime
- **Compliance** — HIPAA-, PCI-, and FedRAMP-aligned solution design for healthcare and federal clients
- **Integration** — CRM, ITSM, and WFM integrations via AppFoundry, REST APIs, and webhook frameworks on AWS, Azure, and GCP

## What I'm building now

Automation and AI tooling on self-hosted infrastructure — n8n workflows, Python utilities, and React/Supabase apps — plus Genesys Cloud CX training content.
